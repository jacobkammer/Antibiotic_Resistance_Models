Current accepted calibration, deterministic transitions, and outcomes

Growth rates (Vera-Yunca et al.): rho_S = 1.47 h^-1 (in vitro); rho_R = 1.323 h^-1 (10% cost); rho_res_S = 0.845 h^-1 (in vivo, mouse lung); rho_res_R = 0.7605 h^-1 (10% cost, same as blood).
Linezolid reservoir/bone penetration = 0.50, the midpoint of the reported 0.40-0.60 range.
Reservoir linezolid efficacy: Emax_L,res = 0.45 x Emax_L = 0.89 h^-1; calibrated, vs Vera-Yunca's in vivo/in vitro ratio 0.303 (independent factor; no longer scaled by rho_res_S/rho_S).
Fixed baselines equal Monte Carlo means: EC50_L = 0.56 mg/L and Emax_L = 1.97 h^-1 (Vera-Yunca et al. in vitro; Hill coefficient kept at 1); k_immune = 0.18 h^-1.
Reservoir carrying capacity B_max_reservoir = 1e7 CFU/mL; reservoir->blood transfer k_r_b = 1e-7 h^-1. Exchange is one-way; the blood->reservoir term (k_b_r) was removed from the model.
Outcome-calibrated sampling distributions:
- EC50_L: mean 0.56 mg/L, CV 0.60, mean-corrected log-normal.
- Emax_L: mean 1.97 h^-1, CV 0.080, mean-corrected log-normal.
- k_immune: mean 0.18 h^-1, CV 0.80, mean-corrected log-normal.

The LOD is a hard extinction boundary at 10 CFU/mL. A subpopulation crossing below the LOD is set to exactly zero and cannot regrow autonomously. Transfer from a detectable reservoir can re-establish blood bacteria when accumulated arrivals reach the LOD.

Bacteremia relapse requires resistant blood below the LOD at linezolid completion followed by resistant blood at or above the LOD during follow-up. Persistent bacteremia at linezolid completion is classified separately as persistent treatment failure. Resolution means neither relapse nor persistent bacteremia.
